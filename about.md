# About
Pieces is a new way of engaging with big issues.
- This is a place to anonymously share your authentic thoughts.
- It's about taking time to be thoughtful about what you think, with quick and easy contributions throughout your day.
- Share your opinion and see your voice reflected in nationwide articles.

## Giving Your Opinion
When the study starts, pieces of articles will appear that you can share your feelings about. These are available for **only a short time**, until the next piece of that article is uploaded, so make sure you check back so you don't miss one.
<br /><br />
New pieces are uploaded daily at **9:00**, **12:00**, **15:00** and **18:00**.
<br /><br />

### 1. Click on a piece on the home page
<img class="help-image" src="/assets/help-images/live-pieces.png" title="Click to enlarge image" alt="The Give Your Opinions section on the home page"/>
<br /><br />

### 2. Read the text while the waiting animation plays
We can all have knee-jerk reactions to things so we've added this time for reflection. Use this pause to consider how you feel about the topic it discusses.
<br /><br />

### 3. Share your opinion and click Enter when you are ready
You can't edit what you enter later, so make sure you are happy with it.
<br /><br />

## Reading The Results
Once all the pieces of a topic have been released, the whole article will be published to Pieces, showing what people are saying. Click on the article in the Your Archived Pieces section on the <a href="/">home page</a> to read it.
<br /><br />
These will usually show at the end of each day, but we also have one topic running across all four days.
<br /><br /> 
<img class="help-image" src="/assets/help-images/archived-pieces.png" title="Click to enlarge image" alt="The Your Archived Pieces section on the home page."/>
<br />
Articles contain actual quotes to represent what people think. We first group lots of opinions together and then select a few quotes to represent those groups. Check to see if you have been included by clicking on the expandable sections in each article:
<br /><br />
<img class="help-image" src="/assets/help-images/collapsible.png" title="Click to enlarge image" alt="An example of expandable sections in an article."/>
<br /><br />

## Notifications
You will get emails during the study when a new piece is available for you to respond to.
<br /><br />
It's important you get these because pieces are only available for a short time.
<br /><br />
If emails are going into your spam folder, please make sure *j.hutchinson4@newcastle.ac.uk* is added to your safe senders list.
<br /><br />

## Taking Part in the Study
Make sure to give your opinion on all the pieces of information you can. This is vital before you can complete the interview after the 4-day study to get the £50 voucher as thanks for your time.
<br /><br />
Once you've completed the 4-day study, we will have the online interview at the time we arrange together.
<br /><br />
All the results from this interview will be anonymised before analysis. Please see the <a href="/assets/documents/Participant Informaton Sheet PSM04.pdf" target="_blank">study description</a> for more information. You should have completed and returned a <a href="/assets/documents/Participant Consent Form PSMC04.pdf" target="_blank">permission form</a> before taking part. If you haven't, please <a href="mailto:j.hutchinson4@newcastle.ac.uk">email me</a>.
